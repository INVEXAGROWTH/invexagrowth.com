Accessibility Statement

Last reviewed: September 7, 2026

Our commitment

Invexa Growth is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for everyone, including people with disabilities. We continually improve our website to align with recognized accessibility standards.

Conformance target

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA, the standard cited by the U.S. Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) and adopted by the European Accessibility Act.

What we do

  • Use semantic HTML so screen readers can navigate our content.
  • Provide meaningful alternative text for images.
  • Ensure sufficient color contrast between text and background.
  • Design layouts that scale on mobile and adapt to zoom.
  • Respect user motion preferences (prefers-reduced-motion).
  • Ensure interactive elements are reachable and operable by keyboard.

Feedback

Accessibility is an ongoing effort, and we welcome your feedback. If you encounter something on our site that isn't accessible to you, or if you have a suggestion for improvement, please email connect@invexagrowth.com and we'll respond as quickly as we can.

Third-party content

Some elements of our website (for example, an embedded booking widget or a third-party analytics tool) are controlled by external providers. We work with vendors whose products meet accessibility standards, but we can't guarantee full conformance of embedded third-party content.
