1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help sites remember your preferences, understand how visitors use the site, and provide certain functionality.

2. Types of cookies we use

Essential cookies

These are required for the site to work. They remember things like your cookie preferences and any settings you've chosen. You can't opt out of these because they're necessary for the site to function.

Analytics cookies (optional)

With your consent, we use analytics tools to understand how visitors interact with our site, which pages get viewed, what content resonates, and how we can improve the experience. This data is aggregated and does not personally identify you.

Marketing cookies (optional)

With your consent, we may use marketing cookies to measure the effectiveness of our advertising and to show relevant content across platforms like Meta or Google.

3. Managing your preferences

When you first visit our site, you'll see a cookie banner asking for your consent. You can:

  • Accept all. allow all cookies including analytics and marketing.
  • Essential only. only cookies required for the site to work.
  • Change your mind anytime. click "Cookie Preferences" in the footer to revisit your choices.

4. Browser controls

You can also manage cookies through your browser settings, most browsers let you view, delete, and block cookies. Note that blocking essential cookies may affect how the site works for you.

5. Do Not Track

Our site honors the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal where technically feasible. If your browser sends a GPC signal, we treat it as an opt-out of the sale or sharing of your personal information under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).
